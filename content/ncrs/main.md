The Plasma HEC has been allocated resource across several machines
within the national compute resource (NCRs) for the transition period
following the closure of Archer2. Specifically we have resouce on

- [Cirrus](https://www.cirrus.ac.uk) - CPU
- [Mary Coombs](https://www.hartree.stfc.ac.uk/technologies/mary-coombs/) - GPU
- [Zenith](https://www.hpc.cam.ac.uk/zenith-launch) - GPU
- [Baskerville](https://www.baskerville.ac.uk) - GPU

Please [get in
touch](mailto:d.dickinson@york.ac.uk?Subject=HEC%20Consortium%20enquiry)
if you are at a consortium member institute, your work falls is in our
scope and you are interested in accessing this resource.
